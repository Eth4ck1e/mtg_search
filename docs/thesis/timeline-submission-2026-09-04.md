## Weekly plan

- **Week 1 (Aug 24 – Aug 30):** Development environment setup; review of baseline retrieval results from prior research phase.

- **Week 2 (Aug 31 – Sep 6):** Begin building the query processing components that translate natural-language user queries into structured database filters and semantic search inputs. **Rough abstract due September 4.**

- **Week 3 (Sep 7 – Sep 13):** Continue development of query processing components.

- **Week 4 (Sep 14 – Sep 20):** Complete query processing components; begin evaluation harness setup. **Final abstract due September 18.**

- **Week 5 (Sep 21 – Sep 27):** Begin systematic evaluation across the four system configurations (baseline, ablation, full system, and external comparator).

- **Week 6 (Sep 28 – Oct 4):** Continue evaluation; build automated reporting scripts to generate results tables and figures.

- **Week 7 (Oct 5 – Oct 11):** Complete the evaluation phase; begin performance optimization work based on measured results.

- **Week 8 (Oct 12 – Oct 18):** Optimization work; prepare presentation materials for the upcoming presentation window.

- **Week 9 (Oct 19 – Oct 25):** **Presentation window begins.** Begin drafting the report using logged data and evaluation results.

- **Week 10 (Oct 26 – Nov 1):** Presentation window continues; continue report drafting.

- **Week 11 (Nov 2 – Nov 8):** Presentation window continues; continue report drafting.

- **Week 12 (Nov 9 – Nov 15):** Presentation window closes; continue report drafting.

- **Week 13 (Nov 16 – Nov 22):** Final report assembly; integrate all methodology, results, and discussion sections.

- **Week 14 (Nov 23 – Nov 29):** Final report revisions based on committee feedback.

- **Week 15 (Nov 30 – Dec 6):** Final revisions and submission. **Final thesis paper due December 4.**

## Notes

- The presentation window (Weeks 9–12) requires a working demonstrable system by mid-October, which drives the compressed schedule for the earlier development phases.
- Report drafting begins in Week 9 rather than in a dedicated writing phase at the end of the term. This is intentional: writing incrementally from a working system produces stronger prose than compressing writing into a final two-week window.
- Optimization work in Weeks 7–8 is the primary buffer if any earlier phase runs long.
